People

Principal Investigator

Prof. Lav Varshney

Director

Prof. Lav R. Varshney is the Della Pietra Infinity Professor and inaugural director of the AI Innovation Institute at Stony Brook University. He is co-founder and CEO of Kocree, Inc., a startup company building novel human-controllable AI for discovery and creativity, and chief scientist of Ensaras, Inc., a startup company focused on AI and wastewater treatment. He holds appointments at RAND Corporation and at Brookhaven National Laboratory. He was previously on the faculty of the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, a visiting scholar at Northwestern's Kellogg School of Management, a principal research scientist at Salesforce Research AI, and a research staff member at IBM Research. He is a former White House staffer, having served on the National Security Council staff as a White House Fellow, where he contributed to national AI and wireless communications policy. His research interests include information and coding theory, statistical signal processing, artificial intelligence, neuroscience, and creativity. He received his B.S. degree from Cornell University and his S.M. and Ph.D. degrees from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.

Postdoctoral Researchers

AI Innovation Institute at Stony Brook University

Matthew Babbitt is a Postdoctoral Associate at the AI Innovation Institute. His mathematical interests intersect with Algebra, Algebraic Geometry, Galois Theory, and Elliptic Curves, while his AI research interests focus on theoretical foundations of Machine Learning as well as applications of LLMs to mathematical creation. His current research program seeks to clarify relationships between various algebras and information lattices for use in Information Lattice Learning. Matthew also writes math contest problems, holding memberships on multiple editorial boards. He is also a founding director on the board of the Upstate New York Math Team nonprofit organization dedicated to coaching high-school students for math contests. Matthew received a B.S. in mathematics from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in 2018 and a Ph.D. in pure mathematics from the University of Texas at Dallas in 2025 through the mentorship of Carlos Arreche.

Babak Hemmatian received a degree in clinical psychology from the University of Tehran in his native Iran before moving to the United States, where he received an M.S. in Computer Science and a Ph.D. in Cognitive Science from Brown University. Previously, he was a Beckman Postdoctoral Fellow at the Beckman Institute for Advanced Science and Technology, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, and the Associate Director of the Center for Brain, Biology and Behavior at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln. He studies how complex reasoning evolves naturally in networks of agents, be they humans, machines, or a combination of the two. To this end, he combines theories from cognitive science and neuroscience with behavioral experiments on one hand, and advanced computational methods on the other. He has applied this integrative perspective to domains ranging from moral reasoning to general intelligence to argumentation on social media.
